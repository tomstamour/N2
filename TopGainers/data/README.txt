The file ./gus_stats_ibkr.txt is a dataframe that contains severall Gap-Up Shorts Events (GUSE) that were traded in the past. I will remember to you the trading strategy/technique of the Gap-Up Short: the commonly used stratgies are to enter Short waiting for a 5 to 10% price Push after market opens at 9:30 (this is done setting a Short Limit Order at 5 to 10% over the Open price), then the exit/Take-profit method is to place a Cover/Buy Limit 15% under the Open price, and also there is always a protection inplace when we are in the Short position using Stop Loss Market Order at 30 to 40% above the Open price.

in this directory I want you to think as a Quantitative Trader and analyse the dataset to help provide the best values to set for Short Entry Limit Order, best values for StopLoss/Cover-Market-Limit, and best values for exit/Take-profit Cover/Buy Limit.
